Frequently asked questions

Short answers for parents and kids curious about Learning Odyssey.

What is Learning Odyssey?

A free web app where kids pick a fantasy world and a learning topic, then play a ten-chapter illustrated adventure shaped by their choices. Science and history questions are woven into the story.

What ages is it for?

Roughly ages 7–12 (elementary / early middle). Younger children can play with a parent; older kids may still enjoy it depending on reading comfort.

Is it free?

Yes. You can try an adventure without creating an account.

Do kids need an account?

No. Guest play works in the browser. Optional Google sign-in is there if you want progress saved beyond this browser session.

How is it different from other AI kids story apps?

Most generate personalized bedtime stories. Learning Odyssey is choose-your-adventure: choices shape the plot, with hundreds of curriculum-style questions across subjects woven into the narrative, plus AI illustrations that evolve with the character.

What subjects can kids explore?

Topics include areas like astronomy, dinosaurs, oceans, ancient civilisations, music and sound, and more — you pick a topic when the adventure begins.
