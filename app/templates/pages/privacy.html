Privacy

Learning Odyssey is a free educational storytelling app aimed at kids roughly ages 7–12. This page explains what we collect in plain language.

This is a straightforward product privacy notice, not formal legal advice. If you need a lawyer-reviewed policy for schools or app stores, say so and we can upgrade it.

Who it’s for

The experience is designed for children roughly ages 7–12. Parents and guardians should decide whether guest play or signed-in play is right for their child.

What you can do without an account

You can try an adventure as a guest. Guest progress is tied to the browser session (and related browser storage). Clearing site data or switching devices typically means that guest progress is lost.

Optional Google sign-in

If you choose Continue with Google, we use Google authentication (via our auth provider) so progress can persist across sessions. We receive basic account identifiers needed to keep you signed in and to associate adventures with your account — not your Google password.

Adventure and learning data

To run the product we store adventure-related information such as the world and topic you chose, chapter progress, choices, and answers needed to continue the story. Generated story text and illustrations are created to deliver the adventure.

Cookies and similar storage

We use cookies or local/session storage for things like keeping a session, remembering guest identifiers, and resuming an in-progress adventure.

Third-party services

The app relies on infrastructure and services such as hosting, authentication, and AI model providers to generate story content and illustrations. Those providers process data as needed to provide their service.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell children’s personal information.
  • We don’t show third-party advertising in the adventure experience.

Contact

Questions about this notice: reach the maintainer via the project repository.
